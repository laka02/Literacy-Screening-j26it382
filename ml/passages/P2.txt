1. On Sunday, I go to a shop with my mother.
2. There are many things, such as books, bags and pencils.
3. My mother looks at the price and asks the seller.
4. “How much is this book?” she asks, and he says, “It is fifty rupees.”
5. We pay the cashier and say “Thank you” to the seller.
6. I am happy because I like shopping with my mother.
